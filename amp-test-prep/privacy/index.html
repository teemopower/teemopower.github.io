Privacy Policy

Who we are

A&P Test Prep — FAA Mechanic is published by Adaptation Apps ("we", "us"). It is an Android test-prep app for the FAA mechanic written exams — General, Airframe and Powerplant (package name com.adaptationapps.amprep). Contact: jimmykim925@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. Your study data — your practice-test answers, scores, bookmarks, progress and settings — is saved only on your device and is not transmitted to us or to any third party. The app works fully offline.

Purchases

Full Access is a one-time in-app purchase (product amp_full_access, $4.99) processed by Google Play Billing. RevenueCat helps verify that purchase: it receives the store transaction identifiers needed to confirm it, such as the transaction ID and product ID, and may process device identifiers for that purpose. RevenueCat does not receive your card details, and neither do we — your payment details go to Google, never to us or to RevenueCat. How Google and RevenueCat handle your information is governed by their own privacy policies.

No ads, no tracking, no sharing

The app shows no ads and contains no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or trackers. We do not sell, share or rent any information, because the app does not send us any information in the first place. The only data that leaves your device in connection with the app is the purchase-verification data described above, handled by Google Play and RevenueCat under their policies.

Permissions

The app does not request any sensitive permissions. It works offline and does not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone or files.

Children

This app is a study aid for adults preparing for FAA mechanic written exams. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases should be made by an adult or with an adult's permission.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to erase all study data stored on your device. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Purchase records are held by Google Play and RevenueCat; contact us if you need help with a purchase.

About the study content

The app's 2,500 practice questions are original questions grounded in public-domain FAA handbooks. They are AI-drafted against those handbooks and spot-checked; they have not been reviewed by a certificated A&P mechanic. The app is an independent study aid, not affiliated with or endorsed by the FAA, and does not reproduce actual FAA test questions. See the Terms of Use for the full disclaimer.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
