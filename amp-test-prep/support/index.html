How the app works

A&P Test Prep is an offline study aid for the FAA mechanic written exams. It contains 2,500 original practice questions — 600 General, 950 Airframe and 950 Powerplant — covering all 40 Airman Certification Standards (ACS) subject areas. Every question comes with an explanation and a citation to the public-domain FAA handbook it is based on (FAA-H-8083-30B, FAA-H-8083-31B, FAA-H-8083-32B, and the ACS, FAA-S-ACS-1). There are no accounts, no sign-in and no ads, and the app works fully offline — no internet connection is needed to study.

Please note: the questions are AI-drafted against the FAA handbooks and spot-checked; they have not been reviewed by a certificated A&P mechanic. The app is an independent study aid, not affiliated with or endorsed by the FAA, does not reproduce actual FAA test questions, and does not guarantee a pass. Use the official FAA handbooks as your authoritative source. See the Terms of Use for the full disclaimer.

Frequently asked questions

What do I get for free, and what does Full Access unlock?

The app is free to download and includes a free sample: the first 50 questions of each test (General, Airframe and Powerplant). Full Access is a one-time $4.99 purchase that unlocks all 2,500 questions across all three tests. It is not a subscription — you pay once.

How do purchases work?

Full Access is bought through Google Play Billing (in-app product amp_full_access) and verified by RevenueCat. Your payment details go to Google — we never see them. There are no subscriptions and no ads.

I bought Full Access but it's not unlocked. How do I restore my purchase?

Use Restore Purchases in the app. Make sure you're signed in to the same Google Play account you bought Full Access with. If it still doesn't unlock, email us at jimmykim925@gmail.com with your Google Play order number and we'll look into it.

Can I study offline?

Yes. The app is offline-first: once installed, all questions, explanations and your progress are stored on your device, and you can study with no internet connection. An internet connection is only needed to buy Full Access.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress and scores are stored only on your device.

What happens to my progress if I delete the app or switch phones?

Deleting the app or clearing its data erases your study progress, because it lives only on your device. Your Full Access purchase is tied to your Google Play account, not to the device, so you can restore it with Restore Purchases on a new device signed in to the same Google Play account.

Are these the real FAA test questions?

No. The questions are original practice questions written for study, grounded in the public-domain FAA handbooks. They do not reproduce actual FAA test questions, and studying them does not guarantee a pass.

I think a question or explanation is wrong. What should I do?

Please tell us — email jimmykim925@gmail.com with the question text (or a screenshot) and the handbook reference you believe is correct. We review reports and fix confirmed errors. Remember the questions have not been reviewed by a certificated A&P mechanic, so always double-check critical material against the current official FAA handbooks.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy — please request a refund through Google Play. We can't issue refunds directly.

Where is my study data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and your answers, scores and settings are never sent to us. Deleting the app erases your study data; purchases can be restored with Restore Purchases. See the Privacy Policy for details.
