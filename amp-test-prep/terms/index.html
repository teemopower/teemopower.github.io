What this app is

A&P Test Prep — FAA Mechanic is an Android study aid for the FAA mechanic written exams — General, Airframe and Powerplant — published by Adaptation Apps ("we", "us"). It offers 2,500 original practice questions across all 40 Airman Certification Standards (ACS) subject areas, each with an explanation and a citation to the public-domain FAA handbook it is based on. The app is for personal study use only.

Free sample and Full Access

The app is free to download and includes a free sample: the first 50 questions of each test (General, Airframe and Powerplant).
