Boba Buddies Mobile

Privacy Policy · Effective November 23, 2026

The short version

  • Boba Buddies Mobile (iOS and Android) has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics or tracking.
  • Your pets, coins, shop items and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • Pet purchases are handled by Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchase, verified through RevenueCat. We don't see your payment details.

Privacy Policy

Who we are

Boba Buddies Mobile is published by Adaptation Apps ("we", "us"). It is available for iOS and Android (package name com.adaptationapps.bobabuddies). Contact: hangrygrogu@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. Your game data — your pets, their growth stages and meters, coins, shop purchases, room backgrounds and settings such as the mute preference — is saved only on your device and is not transmitted to us or to any third party.

Purchases

Extra pets are one-time purchases processed by Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple In-App Purchase (iOS). RevenueCat helps verify those purchases: it receives the store transaction identifiers needed to confirm a purchase, such as the transaction ID and product ID. RevenueCat does not receive your card details, and neither do we — your payment details go to Google or Apple, never to us or to RevenueCat. How Google, Apple and RevenueCat handle your information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Music and sound

The app's music and sound effects are generated inside the app. No audio is recorded, uploaded or sent anywhere.

Permissions

The app may ask permission to send you notifications for care reminders (for example, when a pet is hungry). These reminders are generated on your device from your own game data. Notifications are optional — they only work if you enable them, and you can turn them off at any time in your device settings or in the app. No other permissions are used, and no data leaves your device to make reminders work.

Children

Boba Buddies is a general-audience game. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases should be made by an adult or with an adult's permission.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to erase all game data stored on your device. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Purchase records are held by Google Play or the App Store and RevenueCat; contact us if you need help with a purchase.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
