Frequently asked questions

How do I adopt a pet?

Momo and Kiki are free — they're yours from the start. The other eight pets (Boba, Yuki, Taro, Purin, Sora, Miso, Hana, and Nori) are one-time purchases on the Pals tab, or adopt all eight at once with the Adopt All bundle.

How do purchases work?

Extra pets are one-time purchases made through Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple In-App Purchase (iOS). There are no subscriptions and no ads. Your payment details go to Google or Apple — we never see them.

I bought a pet but it's not showing up. How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Pals tab and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Google Play or Apple account you bought the pet with. If it still doesn't appear, email us at hangrygrogu@gmail.com with your order number and we'll look into it.

My pet is sick. What do I do?

Give your pet Medicine from the Room tab — it costs 10 coins and restores health. If you're short on coins, play one of the three mini-games (Catch the Star, Memory Match, or Jump!) to earn more. Sick pets can still play games and earn full rewards.

My pet is sad and won't play games.

A sad pet needs care first: feed it, give it a drink, bathe it, or let it sleep until its meters recover. Once your pet is happy again, the games unlock.

How do pets grow?

Every pet starts as an egg, which hatches after about 30 seconds. Pets then grow from baby to child to teen to adult, one stage per day. Take great care of your pet and it may grow into a sparkly adult form.

How often should I check in?

About three times a day — morning, lunch, and dinner works well. Your pet's hunger, thirst, energy, happiness, and cleanliness slowly drop over time, and they'll let you know when they need attention.

Can I turn the music or sound off?

Yes. Use the mute control in the app to turn music and sound effects on or off. The setting is saved on your device.

Where is my game data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and your pets, coins, and settings are never sent to us. Deleting the app erases your game data; purchases can be restored with the Restore Purchases button on the Pals tab.

Can I turn off the care reminders?

Yes. The reminders are optional notifications generated on your device. Turn them off any time in your device settings or in the app.
