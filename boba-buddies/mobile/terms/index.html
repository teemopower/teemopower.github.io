What Boba Buddies is

Boba Buddies is a virtual pet game for iOS and Android, published by Adaptation Apps ("we", "us"). You adopt a cute pet that lives on your phone: feed it, play with it, keep it clean and happy, and watch it grow from a baby into an adult. It is a game for fun — it is not a real animal, and it has no effect on your real pets, if you have any.

Pets and purchases

Two starter pets, Momo and Kiki, are free. Eight more pets are available as one-time purchases ($0.99–$1.99 each), or all eight together in the Adopt All bundle ($7.99). Each purchase is one-time, not a subscription — you pay once and the pet is yours. All pets are digital items for use inside the Boba Buddies app only; they have no use or value outside the app. Payments are processed through Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchase, with purchase verification by RevenueCat.

Coins and in-game items
