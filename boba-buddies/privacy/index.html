Boba Buddies

Privacy Policy · Effective November 23, 2026

The short version

  • Boba Buddies has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics or tracking.
  • Your pets, coins, shop items and settings are stored only in your browser. We never receive them.
  • Pet purchases are handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe. We don't see your payment details.

Privacy Policy

Who we are

Boba Buddies is published by Adaptation Apps ("we", "us"). Contact: hangrygrogu@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Your game data — your pets, their growth stages and meters, coins, shop purchases, room backgrounds and settings such as the mute preference — is saved in your browser's local extension storage and is not transmitted to us or to any third party. Boba Buddies contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.

Purchases

Extra pets are one-time purchases processed by ExtensionPay and Stripe. When you buy a pet, ExtensionPay receives your email address so it can identify your purchase and let you restore it later (by a magic sign-in link). Stripe processes the payment; your card details go to Stripe, not to us. How ExtensionPay and Stripe handle your information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Notifications

Boba Buddies can show local reminders (for example, when a pet is hungry) using your browser's notification system. These reminders are generated on your device from your own game data. Nothing is sent anywhere to make them work, and you can turn them off in the extension.

Children

Boba Buddies is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Purchases should be made by an adult or with an adult's permission.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the extension, or clear its browser storage, to delete everything Boba Buddies has stored on your device. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Purchase records are held by ExtensionPay and Stripe; contact us if you need help with a purchase record.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
