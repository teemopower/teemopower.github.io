What Boba Buddies is

Boba Buddies is a virtual pet browser extension. You adopt a cute pet that lives in your browser: feed it, play with it, keep it clean and happy, and watch it grow from a baby into an adult. It is a game for fun — it is not a real animal, and it has no effect on your real pets, if you have any.

Pets and purchases

Two starter pets, Momo and Kiki, are free. Eight more pets are available as one-time purchases ($0.96–$1.99 each), or all eight together in the Adopt All bundle ($7.99). Each purchase is one-time, not a subscription — you pay once and the pet is yours. Payments are processed by ExtensionPay and Stripe. If a purchase doesn't unlock, use Restore Purchases in the extension (it signs you in with your purchase email). For refund questions, contact us at hangrygrogu@gmail.com and we'll help.

Your responsibility

Your game data lives only in your browser. Uninstalling the extension or clearing its storage may permanently lose your pets and progress, so keep your purchase email accessible — that's how you restore pets you bought. Purchases should be made by an adult or with an adult's permission.
