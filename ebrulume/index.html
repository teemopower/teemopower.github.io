Ebrulume: Daily Paper Marbling

A cozy daily ink-and-water puzzle from Adaptation Apps

Today’s water tray is waiting

Drop historical inks, drag a rake or comb through real marbling physics, and lift the paper to reveal your print. Play in a minute or two, check your match, and keep a streak — with one automatic weekly freeze.

No account, no ads, and no energy timers. The daily puzzle, Print Archive, share cards, and timelapse exports are free. An optional one-time Studio Unlock ($3.99) opens Free Play and extra palettes and combs.
