Ebrulume: Daily Paper Marbling

Privacy Policy · Effective November 19, 2026

The short version

  • Ebrulume has no account, no first-party servers, no ads, and no first-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Your prints, streak, settings, and Studio Unlock status are stored only on your device. We do not receive them.
  • Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment card or billing address.
  • If you choose to share a print, card, emoji summary, or timelapse, that file leaves the device through your phone’s share sheet — or, on iOS, can be saved to your photo library if you ask the app to save it.

Privacy Policy

Who we are

Ebrulume (com.adaptationapps.ebrulume) is published by Adaptation Apps ("we", "us"). Contact: hangrygrogu@gmail.com.

This policy describes the mobile apps on iOS and Android, and these support pages hosted at teemopower.github.io/ebrulume.

Information we do not collect

We do not run our own servers for the app. We do not create accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email, contacts, or location in the app. Ebrulume does not include first-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking software.

Information stored on your device

The following is saved in the app’s private storage on your device (for example localStorage, sessionStorage, and IndexedDB). We do not receive it.

WhatWhy
Daily prints in Your Prints (the app keeps up to 60)So you can revisit scores and replay or re-share a print
Streak, theme, mute, haptics, and similar settingsTo remember how you like to play
A local Studio Unlock receipt (product id only)To remember whether Studio features are unlocked on this device
An in-progress tray (temporary)To keep a puzzle you have started during the current session

Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app’s storage in system settings, deletes this local data. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Studio Unlock can still be restored from the Apple ID or Google account that purchased it.

Device backups

On Android, Ebrulume turns off Auto Backup (android:allowBackup="false"). Google’s device backup does not include the app’s local prints, settings, or Studio receipt. Clearing the app or uninstalling it on Android therefore removes that local data.

On iOS, your device’s own backup (for example iCloud Backup) may still include the app’s local data, according to your device settings. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Sharing prints and saving to Photos

If you use Share, you choose where a card, emoji summary, image, or timelapse goes — for example a message, a social app, or Files. That transfer is a user-initiated action through the system share sheet. We do not receive the file.

On iOS, if you choose to save a print or timelapse, the app requests permission only to add media to your photo library (it does not need to read your existing photos). The usage string is: “Save an Ebrulume print or timelapse to your photo library.”

Side-by-side share cards include today’s target pattern next to your print, along with your score and date. The compact emoji summary line (for example ebrulume #47 🌊 87% 🔥5) does not show the target pattern.

Purchases

The optional one-time Studio Unlock is processed by Apple’s StoreKit 2 on iOS, or Google Play Billing Library on Android. The store shares with the app only whether that product is owned. We never see your payment method, billing address, or government ID. Your payment information is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy or the Google Privacy Policy.

When you buy or restore Studio Unlock, or when the billing library contacts the store to check entitlement, the store’s own systems receive the information they need to complete that request. We do not operate a billing server and we do not send purchase tokens or receipts to a backend of ours.

The Android billing library is a Google SDK and may include other Google Play services components as technical dependencies. Ebrulume does not request location permission and does not call location APIs. We do not collect location.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit them, GitHub may process standard server logs such as IP address, date and time, and browser information, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not run our own analytics on these pages.

If you email us

If you write to hangrygrogu@gmail.com, we receive whatever you include in that message (for example your email address and a description of a bug) so we can reply. We use that only to provide support.

Children

Ebrulume is a general-audience puzzle and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share on-device game data with advertisers or analytics companies, because we do not receive that data. The only information that leaves your device in ordinary use is what Apple or Google handle for a purchase or restore you initiate or that the store requires to check entitlement, and anything you choose to send through the system share sheet, Photos, or email.
