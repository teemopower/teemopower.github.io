Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Ebrulume has no account and no servers of ours, so we cannot see or recover your prints or streak. On iOS, if device backup is turned on, your usual backup (for example iCloud Backup) may include the app’s local data. On Android, Auto Backup is turned off, so a Google device backup will not restore local prints, settings, or streak.

I bought Studio Unlock but the extra palettes and combs are still locked

Open Studio Unlock in the app and tap Restore Purchases, while signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. Make sure the device is online for that step.

What does Studio Unlock include?

A one-time $3.99 purchase (or the local equivalent) unlocks Free Play / Studio mode with no daily target, plus the Ottoman Classic, Suminagashi Mist, and Mineral Botanicals palettes, and the Nonpareil, Gelgit, Wave, Double, and Fine combs. The daily puzzle, streak, Print Archive, share cards, and timelapse stay free.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not by Adaptation Apps directly. Use your App Store or Google Play purchase history and their refund request flow.

Do share cards spoil today’s pattern?

The 1:1 and 9:16 side-by-side cards show today’s target next to your print. If you want to share a result without showing the target, use the compact emoji summary line instead (for example ebrulume #47 🌊 87% 🔥5).

My prints or streak disappeared

They are saved only on that device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage resets them. On Android, a Google device backup will not restore them. Studio Unlock can still be restored from the store account that purchased it.
