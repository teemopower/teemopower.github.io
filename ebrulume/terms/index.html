What Ebrulume is

Ebrulume is a daily paper-marbling (ebru) puzzle published by Adaptation Apps. You drop ink, comb the water, and lift a print to match that day’s target pattern. These terms govern your use of the iOS and Android apps.

Free daily play

The daily puzzle, streak tracking, weekly streak freeze, local Print Archive, share cards, emoji summary, and timelapse exports are free. There are no ads, no energy timers, and no paid streak repairs. You do not need an account.

Studio Unlock

Studio Unlock is an optional one-time in-app purchase of $3.99 (or the equivalent in your local currency). It is not a subscription — you pay once and there is nothing to cancel. It unlocks Free Play / Studio mode (no daily target), the Ottoman Classic, Suminagashi Mist, and Mineral Botanicals palettes, and the Nonpareil, Gelgit, Wave, Double, and Fine combs.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account at confirmation of purchase. The product id is studio_unlock. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies. If you restore purchases on a new device while signed in with the same store account, Studio Unlock should unlock again at no extra charge. Use Restore Purchases in the app if the unlock does not appear after reinstalling.

Studio Unlock is not enabled for Family Sharing in the current product configuration. A purchase is tied to the Apple ID or Google account that bought it.
