Fold & Fly

Privacy Policy · Effective November 19, 2026

The short version

  • Fold & Fly has no account, no servers, and no analytics or tracking SDKs of our own.
  • Everything you do in the game — your folds, flights, bests, coins, and upgrades — is stored only on your device. We never receive it.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID to show and measure ads. Buying Pro ($2.99, one time) removes ads entirely.
  • The Pro purchase is handled by Google Play. We don't see your payment details.

Privacy Policy

Who we are

Fold & Fly is published by Adaptation Apps ("we", "us"). Contact: hangrygrogu@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Your folds, flights, best scores, coins, and upgrades are saved in the app's private storage on your device and are not transmitted to us or to any third party. Fold & Fly contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking software of our own.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Fold & Fly displays ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect and share:

  • Your device's Advertising ID, used to show ads, measure their performance, and — where you have not opted out — personalize them.
  • Device and network information such as device model, operating system, approximate location (derived from IP address), and app interaction data, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices.

You can control this on your device: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads lets you delete your Advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization. If you buy Pro, the app shows no ads and AdMob is not loaded, so no advertising data is collected from the app at all.

Device backups

Your phone's operating system may include Fold & Fly's data in your own device backup (for example Google's Android backup), according to your device settings. Those backups are controlled by you and your platform provider, not by us.

Purchases

Fold & Fly Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. The store shares with the app only whether you have an active purchase, through our purchase provider RevenueCat, which receives no game data from the app. Your payment information is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Children

Fold & Fly is intended for general audiences aged 18 and over and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Deleting your data

Use More → Erase all my data in the app, or uninstall the app, to delete everything Fold & Fly has stored on your device. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Ad data held by Google is governed by Google's privacy controls.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
