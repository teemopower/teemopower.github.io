Henledger

Privacy Policy · Effective November 19, 2026

The short version

  • Henledger has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Everything you enter — birds, egg counts, notes, photos — is stored only on your device. We never receive it.
  • The Pro purchase is handled by Google Play. We don't see your payment details.

Privacy Policy

Who we are

Henledger is published by Adaptation Apps ("we", "us"). Contact: hangrygrogu@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Your birds, egg counts, notes, and photos are saved in the app's private storage on your device and are not transmitted to us or to any third party. Henledger contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking software.

Photos

Photos you choose in Henledger are stored on your device as part of your birds' profiles. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Device backups and exports

Your phone's operating system may include Henledger's data in your own device backup (for example Google's Android backup), according to your device settings. Those backups are controlled by you and your platform provider, not by us. If you choose to export a CSV or backup file, you decide where that file is shared.

Purchases

Henledger Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. The store shares with the app only whether you have an active purchase, through our purchase provider RevenueCat, which receives no bird, egg, or note data from the app. Your payment information is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Children

Henledger is intended for adults keeping backyard chickens and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Deleting your data

Use More → Erase all data in the app, or uninstall the app, to delete everything Henledger has stored on your device. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
