What Henledger is

Henledger is a simple egg and flock log for hobby backyard chicken keepers: a daily egg counter, a bird roster with photos and dates, notes, egg charts, and CSV export. It is a personal record-keeping tool for hobby keepers — not for breeders or farm businesses, and not a veterinary, animal-health, accounting, or legal service. Henledger gives no advice about chicken health, disease, breeding, or feed, and nothing in the app should be treated as such advice. If a bird is unwell, consult a veterinarian.

Henledger Pro

Henledger Pro is a one-time purchase of $7.99 (or the equivalent in your local currency) that unlocks unlimited birds, photos, full egg charts, CSV export, and full history. It is not a subscription — you pay once and there is nothing to cancel. Payment is charged to your Google Play account at confirmation of purchase. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its policies. If you restore purchases on a new device signed into the same store account, Pro unlocks again at no extra charge.

Free tier

The free version of Henledger covers one coop of up to 6 birds and the last 14 days of history. Older history, additional birds, photos, full charts, and CSV export require Henledger Pro.

Your responsibility

You're responsible for the accuracy of what you enter — bird details, egg counts, dates, notes, and photos — and for keeping your device secure. Because your data lives only on your device, losing or resetting the device without a backup may permanently lose your flock records. Use the backup export in More regularly if your records matter to you.
