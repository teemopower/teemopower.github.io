Likensy

Draw the picture. Nail the likeness.

Likensy is a drawing game. Each level shows a simple picture: an apple, a house, a cat. You rebuild it with shapes that snap neatly into place, then tap Done. Get the parts roughly right and you win; get them close and you earn three stars.

Make your own picture and send it to a friend as a link. They can play it right away, even without the app. Trade pictures every day to grow a friend streak.

Get it on Google Play

Help

How do I win? Every part of the target needs a matching shape in the same colour, in roughly the right place and size. Tap Done to check; the game names anything still missing.

How do friend streaks work? A day counts when you and a friend both send each other a picture. One missed day a week is covered by a free freeze. Streaks are kept on your phone.
