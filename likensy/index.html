likensy

copy the pic. send it. keep the streak.

Likensy is a drawing game. You see a picture, like an apple, a pig, a little creature or a stick figure your friend drew, and you copy it with your finger. The game scores how alike the two drawings are. Get 65% or more to pass, and get closer for more stars.

Draw your own picture and send it to a friend as a link. They can play it right away, even without the app. Send each other a picture every day to grow a friend streak.

get it on google play

help

How is my drawing scored? Both drawings are scaled to the same size, then the game checks how much of the original your lines cover and how much of your drawing sits on the original's lines. A bit of wobble is fine. Drawing smaller or off to one side is fine. Leaving out a part or adding lots of extra lines costs points. If a whole part is missing, you can still pass but only with one star. Two or more missing parts is a fail.

Do I have to draw everything by hand? Mostly, yes. There are ready-made shapes under "shapes +" if you want a perfect circle or box.

How do friend streaks work? A day counts when you and a friend both send each other a picture. One missed day a week is covered by a free freeze. Streaks are kept on your phone.
