Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026. Likensy is made by Adaptation Apps.

The short version

Likensy does not collect personal data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress, drawings and friend streaks stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Stars, your current drawings, saved Studio drawings, challenges you receive, friend streaks, your settings and the first name you choose to show friends. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Challenge links

When you send a challenge, the picture is packed into the link itself, along with your first name if you entered one and your streak count with that friend. The link goes wherever you choose to share it, through your phone's share sheet. We do not run a server that receives or stores challenges. The web player at teemopower.github.io/likensy/c/ reads the drawing from the link inside your browser; it is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs.

Purchases

Likensy Studio is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we only receive confirmation that the purchase exists so it can be unlocked. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles payment data.

Install link

If you install Likensy from a friend's challenge, the app reads the challenge from Google Play's install referrer once, so it can open that picture. Nothing is sent to us.

Notifications

If you turn on the streak reminder, your device schedules a local notification. No notification service of ours is involved.

Children

Likensy is rated for ages 13 and up. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.
