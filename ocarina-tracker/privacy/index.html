Privacy Policy — Ocarina Tracker

Last updated: November 19, 2026
Developer: Adaptation Apps
Contact: hangrygrogu@gmail.com

Ocarina Tracker is an unofficial fan checklist app for the Ocarina of Time remake on Nintendo Switch 2. It helps players track collectibles and completion progress. This policy explains what information the app collects — in short: we collect no personal information.

Information we collect

No personal information. Ocarina Tracker does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us or to any third party. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no in-app purchases.

Checklist content updates

When the app launches, and when you tap “Check for content updates” in About, it downloads the latest public checklist content (plain text files) from GitHub Pages at teemopower.github.io/ocarina-tracker-content. That request sends no personal information — no account details, no device identifiers, no location, and nothing about your checklist progress. The downloaded content is stored on your device so the app works offline. If the download fails or the content looks wrong, the app keeps using the checklist built into it.

Information stored on your device

Your checklist progress — which items you have checked off, your Young / Adult era setting, and any boss rematch times you type in — is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also erase it from inside the app under About > Reset all progress. Downloaded checklist content is also stored on your device, as described above.

Third-party services

GitHub Pages hosts the public checklist content files the app downloads for content updates (see above). That is the app’s only network connection. The app includes no analytics, advertising libraries, or other third-party SDKs.

Children’s privacy

Ocarina Tracker is a general-audience checklist app. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children either. Parents or guardians with questions can contact us at the email above.

Unofficial fan app

Ocarina Tracker is an unofficial fan-made app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Nintendo. All artwork, icons, and checklist text in the app are original or fan-written.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and note the new date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
