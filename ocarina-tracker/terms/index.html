What this app is

Ocarina Tracker is an unofficial fan-made checklist companion for the Ocarina of Time remake on Nintendo Switch 2. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Nintendo. All artwork, icons, and checklist text in the app are original or fan-written. The app is provided free of charge.

Use of the app

You may use the app to track your own progress through the game. Your checklist progress is stored only on your device. The app has no accounts and no sign-in. The app checks for updated checklist content online and downloads it when available; it works offline with the checklist included in the app.

Accuracy

The checklists are fan-written and provided as-is. Most entries are checked against post-launch guides for the remake, but a few spots in the remake’s brand-new content are best-effort and may be incomplete or change as more of the remake is mapped. The app is a companion to the game, not a replacement for it, and we do not guarantee that every entry is correct or complete.
