pipe down. privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Short version: Pipe Down reads your notifications on your phone so it can apply the rules you make. It has no internet permission. Nothing it reads is sent to us or to anyone else.

What Pipe Down reads

When you turn on notification access, Android lets Pipe Down see the notifications other apps post: the app that sent it, the title (often a person or chat name), the text, and the time. Pipe Down uses this only to decide whether one of your rules applies and to carry that rule out, for example by dismissing, delaying, hiding or re-alerting the notification, or by sending a reply you wrote.

What is stored, and where

  • Your rules are saved on your phone.
  • The feed, a list of the most recent 300 notifications and what Pipe Down did with each, is saved on your phone. Notifications covered by a "hide" rule are saved without their text.
  • Both are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

What leaves your phone

Nothing. Pipe Down does not request the internet permission, so it cannot upload your notifications, rules or feed. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never receive your data, so we cannot share or sell it.

One feature sends a message on your behalf: a "reply for me" rule sends the text you wrote through the other app's own reply action, to the person who messaged you. That message is handled by that app, under its privacy policy.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. Pipe Down only learns whether the unlock is owned, and keeps that answer on your phone.

Permissions

  • Notification access: to read notifications and act on them. You turn it on yourself and can turn it off at any time in Android settings.
  • Show notifications: for the copies shown by "hide" and "undo" rules.
  • Vibrate: for vibration patterns.
  • Google Play billing: for the optional unlock.

The flashlight is used by "flash the light" rules; this does not use the camera to take pictures.

Children

Pipe Down is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
