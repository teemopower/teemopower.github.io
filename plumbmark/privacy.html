Plumbmark privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Short version: Plumbmark takes photos and stamps them with your location, direction and time. Your photos and readings stay on your phone. Plumbmark has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics. We never receive your data.

What Plumbmark uses

  • Camera: to take the photos you ask for.
  • Location (precise and approximate): to put coordinates, accuracy, altitude, address and true-north heading on your photos. Location is read only while the camera screen is open. You can refuse it and still take photos without a position.
  • Motion and compass sensors: for heading, tilt and roll. These need no permission.

What is stored, and where

  • Your photos, the unstamped originals if you keep them, your job names, notes, the readings for each photo, your settings and your logo are saved in Plumbmark's storage on your phone.
  • If "Also save to the phone's gallery" is on, copies are saved to your phone's Pictures/Plumbmark folder, where your gallery and any backup service you use can see them.
  • Readings are also written inside each photo file (EXIF), so anyone you send a photo to can read where and when it was taken. Turn fields off in Stamp fields if you don't want them printed; the readings in the file stay so the photo remains useful as proof.
  • Everything in Plumbmark's own storage is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Street addresses

To show a street address, Plumbmark asks your phone's built-in address lookup (Android's Geocoder) for the nearest address. On most phones that lookup is provided by Google and needs a connection; Google handles it under its own privacy policy. If no lookup is available, Plumbmark simply leaves the address out.

What leaves your phone

Only what you choose to share. When you tap Share, export a ZIP or make a PDF report, the file goes to the app you pick (mail, messages, Drive and so on), and that app's privacy policy applies. Plumbmark itself cannot upload anything: it does not request the internet permission.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro unlock is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. Plumbmark only learns whether Pro is owned, and keeps that answer on your phone.

Children

Plumbmark is a work tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
