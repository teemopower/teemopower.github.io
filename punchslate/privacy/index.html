Punchslate

Privacy Policy · Effective November 18, 2026

The short version

  • Punchslate has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Everything you enter — jobs, clients, photos, voice notes, prices, invoices — is stored only on your device. We never receive it.
  • The Pro purchase is handled by Google Play (and the App Store on iOS). We don't see your payment details.

Privacy Policy

Who we are

Punchslate is published by Adaptation Apps ("we", "us"). Contact: hangrygrogu@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Your jobs, clients, photos, voice notes, price book, time logs and invoices are saved in the app's private storage on your device and are not transmitted to us or to any third party. Punchslate contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.

Photos and voice notes

Photos you take or choose in Punchslate are stored on your device as part of your job records. Voice notes are recorded and stored on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Device backups and exports

Your phone's operating system may include Punchslate's data in your own device backup (for example Google's Android backup or iCloud Backup), according to your device settings. Those backups are controlled by you and your platform provider, not by us. If you choose to export a PDF, CSV or backup file, you decide where that file is shared.

Purchases

Punchslate Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play (or the Apple App Store on iOS). The store shares with the app only whether you have an active purchase, through our purchase provider RevenueCat, which receives no job or client data from the app. Your payment information is governed by Google's or Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Punchslate is intended for adults running a trade business and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase all data in the app, or uninstall the app, to delete everything Punchslate has stored on your device. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
