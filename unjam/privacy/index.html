Unjam: Tap Out Arrow Escape

Privacy Policy · Last updated November 7, 2026

This policy explains what information the Unjam mobile game ("the app", "we") handles. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Information we do not collect

Unjam has no accounts, sign-in, chat or social features. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information, and we do not run our own servers that receive data from the app.

Information stored on your device

Your level progress, remaining hints and whether you bought "Remove ads" are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Purchases can be restored from Google Play.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, general device and app information, and ad interactions. Depending on your choices and where you live, ads may be personalized. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from "Privacy choices" on the home screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob and Google Play handle as described above.

Children

Unjam is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Because we store nothing on our own servers, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove local game data, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
