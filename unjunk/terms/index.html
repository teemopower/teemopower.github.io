What Unjunk is

Unjunk is a free browser extension that blocks ads and trackers, dismisses cookie-consent banners, and skips sponsor segments in YouTube videos. It has one on/off switch. By installing and using Unjunk, you agree to these terms.

Free to use

Unjunk is free. There are no accounts, no subscriptions, and no payments in this version. We may add optional paid features in the future; if we do, these terms will be updated and the free features you already have will stay free.

What Unjunk does not promise

  • Not every ad will be blocked. Websites change constantly, and some ads — especially ads served from the same domain as the site itself, and some YouTube ads — may still appear.
  • Some sites may look or behave differently. Blocking requests and dismissing banners can occasionally break a page feature or hide something you wanted to see. If a site misbehaves, turn Unjunk off for that visit.
  • Sponsor skipping depends on a third-party community database (SponsorBlock). If that service is down, or if nobody has marked the sponsor segments in a video, nothing will be skipped.
  • Cookie choices are your responsibility. Unjunk clicks reject/decline options on consent banners when it can find them, but it cannot guarantee a particular site's consent settings, and it never accepts tracking on your behalf.
