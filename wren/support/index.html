Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Wren has no account and no servers, so we can't see or recover your entries.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Wren's data is included in your normal device backup (iCloud Backup or Android backup) if backups are on. Restore that backup to your new phone.

How do I restore Wren Plus?

Open Wren Plus in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions.

How do I delete my data?

Use You → Erase all data in the app, or uninstall Wren.
